**Q15: What is Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)?**
A: RRF is a fusion method used to combine rankings from different search retrievers by calculating `score = sum(1 / (60 + rank))`, merging keyword and semantic results cleanly.

**Q20: Why is chunking important in a RAG pipeline?**
A: Chunking breaks large documents into manageable segments (200-300 words) so retrievers can pinpoint exact passages and fit them within LLM token window limits.

**Q21: What is cosine similarity used for in dense retrieval?**
A: Cosine similarity measures the cosine of the angle between two vectors, helping determine how semantically close a user query vector is to stored document chunk vectors.
